## What is still simulated, and why

- **Households, plots and repayment histories.** No open dataset publishes named smallholders' loan repayments, and none should. Households are simulated *inside real communes*: each household's yields are drawn around that commune's official yields, and the default rate of the simulated loan outcomes is calibrated to BCEAO's 9.4% portfolio degradation rate for Benin's microfinance sector.
- **Plot sizes and crop mixes** come from the World Bank / INSAE EHCVM 2021/22 survey (8,032 Benin households) when `python -m agricredit.opendata ehcvm` has been run: each simulated household's crops and plot sizes are drawn from its department's weighted distributions. Without it, an assumed distribution is used.
- **Officers** are fictional.
